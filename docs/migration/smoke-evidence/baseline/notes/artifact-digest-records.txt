the advisory source map is RECORDED like the other five but is NOT one of the
  compared five and is not counted in the verdict, because it embeds file paths and
  is only comparable when both sides build from the same relative path.  It is
  recorded so that the adjudication record can print its measured digest, which is
  what lets a reader CHECK that scoping decision instead of taking it on trust:
  60ea8616dfa29ed8cc57910a838024d3172be23693d378b2610adcde68159762  application.min.js.map

why the schema is validated on READ-BACK rather than trusted at the point of
  writing.  Records committed by an earlier revision of this deliverable carried
  a path-qualified label that no producer could emit, which proved they had been
  edited after capture, and nothing in the reader looked at the label at all.
  Two captures whose records label the same artefact differently cannot be
  compared by key.  The label is now asserted, so an edited record is a
  detected condition instead of an invisible one.

a malformed record is NOT the same as an absent one: absence means the artefact
  was not built and the remedy is to build it, whereas malformation means the
  record cannot be trusted and the remedy is to re-capture it.  This program
  refuses both.
