the configured window must SPAN the documented startup time.  The first
  container startup takes 5 to 10 minutes, and the default here is 60
  attempts at 10-second intervals, which is 10 minutes.  Polling stops the
  moment the stack answers, so a fast start costs nothing.

why a single immediate attempt was wrong, recorded because the correction
  is the substance of this note: one probe fired at second zero against a
  stack that will not answer for several minutes fails, every flow behind
  it records a transport failure, and two runs of that shape produce two
  identical directories full of no-response tokens.  A comparison of those
  two reports a clean match while having exercised nothing at all.

readiness does not ABORT the run: each flow still records its own evidence,
  so an unreachable stack produces an explicit, comparable capture rather
  than a truncated one.  It is a recorded PRECONDITION instead: when this
  state reads NEVER-READY, no flow observation in this capture may be read
  as behavioural agreement, however neatly the two directories diff.
