set, and the canonical projection of the .out record set.  All three,

response bodies, transport diagnostics and response headers are not
projected: a body carries generated identifiers by construction.  What
makes a body assertable is a NAMED CHECK over it, and every such check
lands in the .status file, which is compared here in full.

WHAT IS NO LONGER ADVISORY, and why it changed.  An earlier revision of
this comparison read ONE STRING per flow: the final verdict line.  Two
runs can agree on that string while disagreeing about the role granted at
login, the MIME type the repository stored, the ranking the index
returned, the payload the broker carried, the number that was generated,
the task the engine created and the queue the object moved to — every one
of which is behaviour this migration promises to preserve.  Flow 6 in the
previous committed evidence demonstrated it: its two status record sets
differed by two lines and the comparison reported the flow as a match.
The .status record sets and the projected .out record sets are therefore
REQUIRED rows now, and a difference in either is counted.

REQUIRED-DIFFERENCES: 3

How to read that number.  Zero means the two captures agree on every
required row.  Non-zero does NOT by itself mean a regression: it means
something differs and therefore has to be ACCOUNTED FOR, either by being
fixed or by being registered as an accepted deviation with evidence, in
docs/migration/ambiguity-resolutions.md and
docs/migration/pre-existing-defects.md.  A difference that appears in
neither register is an unexplained difference, and that is the condition
this file exists to make impossible to miss.
