the eight application flows - why every one of them recorded SKIPPED, and what
that record is and is not

SUMMARY
All eight flows recorded an explicit SKIPPED verdict with a reason, in both
captures.  None was silently omitted.  A SKIPPED verdict is NOT a pass, and the
comparator does not treat eight matching SKIPPED verdicts as behavioural
agreement: it reported INCONCLUSIVE-STACK-NEVER-READY for exactly that reason,
even though all thirteen required comparisons agreed.

WHY THEY COULD NOT RUN
The flows drive a DEPLOYED application.  Executing them requires two things that
do not exist on this host:
  1. A deployed web archive on a servlet container.  The reactor builds
     successfully and the archive can be produced, but nothing here deploys or
     starts a container.
  2. The six-service reference stack the flows transact against - the search
     engine, the message broker, the content repository, the reporting server,
     the configuration server and the document viewer.  No such services are
     running and no images for them are present.
Readiness polling consequently observed no HTTP response at all, recorded
NEVER-READY, and each flow recorded the transport failure and its own SKIPPED
record with the endpoint it could not reach.

WHY NOTHING WAS SYNTHESISED IN THEIR PLACE
Writing plausible flow results without running the flows would fabricate evidence.
The governing evidence rule forbids asserting an outcome no run produced, and the
baseline-behaviour rule makes an unrun flow's outcome unknowable rather than
assumable.  An absence recorded as an absence is honest and comparable; an absence
filled in with a likely-looking result is worse than no evidence, because it would
be indistinguishable from the real thing and would be trusted.

WHAT THE FLOW CAPTURES DO STILL EVIDENCE
Each flow's three files record the endpoint, the method, the auth mode, the
observed transport outcome and the reason it could not proceed.  That is enough to
verify the flow definitions themselves: the request each flow makes, the read-back
it performs, and the assertion it would apply.  The endpoint paths were read out of
the application's own request mappings and are recorded in the captures, so a
reviewer can confirm the contracts without a running stack.

WHAT REMAINS TO BE CAPTURED, AND HOW
On a host with the reference stack and a deployed archive, run this script twice
with no modification to its body:
  - supply the credential through ARKCASE_PASSWORD_FILE
  - supply the certificate authority through SMOKE_CA_BUNDLE, so verification
    stays on and the credential is actually sent
  - supply the container log through CATALINA_LOG, which is the only way the
    startup frontend build and the process-engine initialisation can be observed
  - point SMOKE_COMPARE_WITH at the other capture on the second run
The eight flows will then replace these SKIPPED records with observations, and the
comparator will compute a verdict over them.

EVIDENCE THAT IS COMPLETE IN THESE CAPTURES
Not everything here is pending.  The following was captured for real and is
committed:
  - the five frontend artifact digests, from two genuinely different builds, with
    the byte-identical result and its provenance and limits recorded in
    notes/frontend-comparison-provenance.txt
  - the toolchain banners for both sides, captured from the tools themselves
  - the per-module unit-test reports under surefire/, emitted by the test runner
    on each runtime rather than derived
  - the corpus figures, measured at run time
  - the reference-stack probes, recording that none of the six answered
